5 · Cross-browser streaming

Two browsers or devices on the same LAN. One publishes the camera or the screen; the other views. The lab server only relays offer / answer / ICE candidates for a room code. The live stats panel shows codec, resolution, fps, bitrate, packets lost, RTT and the candidate pair types.

This page is not a secure context, so camera and screen are blocked (viewing still works). On another device, open the lab over HTTPS; see the README.
