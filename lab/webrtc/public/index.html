DagleiTV WebRTC lab

An independent laboratory for testing browser WebRTC capabilities. It does not use the app's code. Every page shows a capability report and can copy it as JSON.

  • 1 · Camera capture
    getUserMedia, permission flow, devices, constraints, track settings and capabilities.
  • 2 · Screen capture
    getDisplayMedia, audio, displaySurface, track ended; unsupported browsers reported cleanly.
  • 3 · P2P connection
    Loopback and manual copy/paste SDP between two tabs or browsers; ICE candidates and state timeline.
  • 4 · Codec inspection
    Sender/receiver capabilities, MediaCapabilities, setCodecPreferences, negotiated codec.
  • 5 · Cross-browser streaming
    Publish camera or screen to another browser on the LAN through the lab's signaling; live stats.
